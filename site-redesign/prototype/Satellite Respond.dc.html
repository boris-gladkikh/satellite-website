About

Satellite Respond is the downtempo and IDM project of Boris Gladkill. The music drifts between slow-motion soundscapes and intricate, glitching rhythm work, built from processed field recordings, analog synthesis and patient, unresolved melodies.

Each release is framed as a transmission: a fragment picked up from somewhere far away, cold at the edges and warm at the core. The project takes its cues from science fiction, orbital mechanics and the long silences between stations.
